Document Management Assessment

We begin by assessing your existing document repositories, workflows, governance policies and collaboration platforms. This assessment identifies duplication, inconsistent document structures, compliance risks and opportunities where Artificial Intelligence can significantly improve efficiency.

Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)

Many business processes rely on manually reviewing invoices, contracts, forms and technical documentation. We design Intelligent Document Processing solutions that automatically classify documents, extract key information and route content to the appropriate business systems, reducing manual effort while improving accuracy.

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Data Extraction

Modern OCR technologies combined with Artificial Intelligence can transform scanned documents and images into searchable, structured information. We help organisations automate the extraction of names, dates, invoice numbers, contract clauses and other critical business data from both digital and paper documents.

AI Document Classification and Metadata

Artificial Intelligence can automatically classify documents, assign metadata, identify document types and organise information according to business rules. This improves document retrieval, simplifies governance and creates a consistent information architecture across the organisation.

Document Governance and Compliance

Effective document management requires clear governance. We establish document ownership, version control, retention policies, access permissions and audit capabilities to ensure information remains accurate, secure and compliant with regulatory requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Document Management?

AI Document Management uses Artificial Intelligence to automate document classification, information extraction, metadata generation, enterprise search and document lifecycle management, reducing manual work while improving accessibility and governance.

What is Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)?

Intelligent Document Processing combines Artificial Intelligence, Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and machine learning to automatically read, classify and extract information from structured and unstructured documents.

Can AI work with SharePoint and Microsoft 365?

Yes. Artificial Intelligence solutions can integrate with Microsoft SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365 and other enterprise content management platforms to improve document retrieval, governance and collaboration while respecting existing security permissions.

How does AI improve compliance?

AI supports compliance by automatically classifying documents, applying retention policies, monitoring document versions, improving auditability and helping organisations maintain consistent governance across document repositories.

Why is document management important for enterprise AI?

Enterprise AI solutions depend on high-quality information. Well-managed documents enable AI assistants, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions and generative AI platforms to produce more accurate, trustworthy and context-aware responses.
