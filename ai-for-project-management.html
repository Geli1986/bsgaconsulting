How AI Supports the Project Lifecycle

Project Initiation

AI assists with business case preparation, stakeholder analysis, lessons learned reviews and project charter development, helping teams begin projects with greater clarity and consistency.

Project Planning

Artificial Intelligence supports schedule creation, work breakdown structures, risk identification, resource planning and estimation by analysing historical project information and recommending improvements.

Project Execution

Project teams can automate meeting summaries, action tracking, documentation, communications and reporting while receiving AI-assisted recommendations throughout delivery.

Monitoring and Control

AI enables continuous monitoring of project health, schedule performance, resource utilisation, issue trends and delivery risks, allowing project managers to make faster and more informed decisions.

Project Closure

Lessons learned, project documentation, knowledge capture and post-project reviews can all be accelerated using Artificial Intelligence, helping organisations continuously improve future project delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will AI replace Project Managers?

No. AI supports project managers by automating repetitive activities and providing better insights, allowing them to focus on leadership, communication and decision-making.

Which project management activities benefit most from AI?

Reporting, scheduling, meeting summaries, risk management, documentation, stakeholder communications, lessons learned and knowledge management are among the areas where AI delivers the greatest value.

Can AI integrate with Microsoft Project and Microsoft 365?

Yes. Organisations can integrate AI capabilities with Microsoft 365, Microsoft Copilot and other project management platforms to improve collaboration and productivity.
