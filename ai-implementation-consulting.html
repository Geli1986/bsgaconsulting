Why AI Projects Fail

Research consistently shows that many AI initiatives fail to achieve their expected outcomes. The reasons are rarely technical. They usually include:

  • No clear business strategy
  • Poor project governance
  • Weak stakeholder engagement
  • Low user adoption
  • Poor quality data
  • Undefined success metrics
  • Lack of executive sponsorship

Successful AI implementation requires leadership, governance and disciplined execution.
