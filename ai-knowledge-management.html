Knowledge Assessment

Enterprise Search Strategy

Employees often waste valuable time searching across multiple systems for documents, policies and technical information. We design enterprise search solutions that connect knowledge sources, improve discoverability and provide fast, accurate access to trusted information.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Modern AI assistants deliver better results when they retrieve information directly from trusted organisational knowledge rather than relying solely on pre-trained models. Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Knowledge Management?

AI Knowledge Management combines Artificial Intelligence with structured knowledge management practices to help organisations organise, search, retrieve and use information more effectively across the enterprise.

What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) enables AI models to retrieve information from trusted organisational sources before generating responses. This improves accuracy, reduces hallucinations and ensures answers are based on current business information.

Can AI search SharePoint and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Modern enterprise AI solutions can securely access platforms such as SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365 and other authorised repositories, subject to appropriate permissions and governance.

How do you protect confidential information?

We design governance frameworks that include role-based access controls, document permissions, information classification, audit capabilities and responsible AI policies to ensure sensitive information remains protected.

Where should organisations start?

Most organisations benefit from beginning with an AI Readiness Assessment and a Knowledge Management Assessment. These provide a clear understanding of current capabilities, governance gaps and the highest-value opportunities for AI adoption.
