Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Productivity Consulting?

AI Productivity Consulting helps organisations improve employee performance by integrating Artificial Intelligence into everyday work. This includes automating repetitive tasks, improving collaboration, enhancing knowledge access and supporting better decision-making.

How can AI improve employee productivity?

Artificial Intelligence can draft documents, summarise meetings, prioritise tasks, answer questions, retrieve organisational knowledge, automate routine communications and reduce time spent on administrative activities.

Is Microsoft Copilot enough to improve productivity?

Microsoft Copilot is a powerful productivity tool, but achieving lasting improvements also requires well-structured information, effective governance, employee training and redesigned business processes. Technology alone rarely delivers sustainable productivity gains.

How do you measure productivity improvements?

We define measurable KPIs such as time savings, process cycle times, employee adoption, task completion rates, collaboration efficiency and business outcomes to evaluate the impact of AI initiatives.

Will AI require major organisational change?

Successful AI adoption usually involves changes to ways of working, but these can be introduced progressively. We support organisations through structured change management programmes that encourage adoption while minimising disruption.
