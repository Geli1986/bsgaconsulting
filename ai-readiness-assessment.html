AI Readiness Assessment

Understand whether your organisation is ready to implement Artificial Intelligence successfully through a structured assessment of strategy, governance, technology, data and organisational capability.

Why an AI Readiness Assessment Matters

Many organisations invest in Artificial Intelligence because competitors are doing so or because they see impressive demonstrations of new technologies. However, successful AI implementation depends far less on the technology itself than on the organisation's ability to adopt, govern and integrate it effectively. Without a clear understanding of current capabilities, businesses risk investing in projects that fail to deliver measurable value, exceed budgets or face resistance from employees. An AI Readiness Assessment provides an objective evaluation of your organisation before major investment decisions are made. It identifies strengths, highlights capability gaps and establishes practical priorities for successful implementation.

At BSGA Consulting, our assessments focus on business outcomes rather than technology alone. We examine the strategic, operational and organisational factors that determine whether Artificial Intelligence initiatives will succeed over the long term.

Our AI Readiness Framework

Our assessment follows a structured framework that evaluates the capabilities organisations need before implementing Artificial Intelligence successfully. Rather than focusing only on technology, we assess the complete business environment to identify opportunities, constraints and implementation priorities.

1. Business Strategy

Artificial Intelligence should always support business objectives rather than becoming an isolated technology initiative. We assess how AI aligns with your strategic priorities, competitive position and long-term vision.

2. Leadership and Governance

Executive sponsorship is one of the strongest predictors of successful AI adoption. We evaluate governance structures, decision-making processes, accountability and leadership commitment.

3. Data Readiness

Reliable data is the foundation of every successful AI solution. We review data quality, availability, ownership, governance, security and accessibility to determine whether your organisation can support AI initiatives effectively.

4. Technology and Infrastructure

We assess your existing systems, cloud capabilities, software architecture, cybersecurity controls and technology landscape to determine whether your infrastructure is capable of supporting Artificial Intelligence projects.

5. People and Culture

Successful AI implementation depends on people. We evaluate organisational culture, employee engagement, digital skills, training requirements and change management capability to understand how prepared your workforce is for AI adoption.

AI Maturity Levels

Every organisation is at a different stage of its AI journey. Our assessment identifies your current level of maturity and provides practical recommendations for progressing to the next stage.

Level 1 – Initial

Artificial Intelligence is being explored but there is no formal strategy, governance or implementation plan.

Level 2 – Developing

The organisation has identified potential AI opportunities but lacks consistent governance, prioritisation and organisational readiness.

Level 3 – Defined

AI initiatives are aligned with business strategy and supported by structured governance, project management and executive sponsorship.

Level 4 – Managed

AI projects are measured against business outcomes, supported by mature governance and integrated into operational processes.

Level 5 – Optimised

Artificial Intelligence has become a strategic capability supported by continuous improvement, innovation and data-driven decision making across the organisation.
