Frequently Asked Questions

What is Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an Artificial Intelligence assistant integrated into Microsoft applications such as Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Teams. It helps employees create content, analyse information, automate repetitive tasks and improve collaboration.

Do we need to prepare Microsoft 365 before deploying Copilot?

Yes. Preparing your Microsoft 365 environment is essential. Organisations should review SharePoint structures, Teams workspaces, document governance, permissions and information quality before large-scale deployment.

Can Microsoft Copilot access confidential information?

Copilot respects the permissions already configured within Microsoft 365. If permissions are not properly managed, users may gain access to information they should not see. This is why governance and permission reviews are a critical part of every implementation.

How long does Microsoft Copilot implementation take?

The implementation timeline depends on organisational size, Microsoft 365 maturity and governance readiness. Many organisations begin with a pilot programme before expanding adoption across departments.

How do you measure success?

We define measurable success indicators such as employee adoption, time savings, productivity improvements, document quality, meeting efficiency and overall business outcomes to demonstrate the return on investment from Microsoft Copilot.
