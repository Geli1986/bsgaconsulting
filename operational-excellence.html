FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Operational excellence questions.

What is operational excellence consulting?

Operational excellence consulting helps organisations improve how work is performed by identifying process inefficiencies, strengthening performance management, improving ways of working and establishing continuous improvement practices.

How can operational excellence improve business performance?

Operational excellence can improve performance by simplifying processes, reducing inefficiencies, clarifying accountability, improving performance visibility and creating more consistent ways of working.

Can BSGA Consulting help improve business processes?

Yes. BSGA Consulting helps organisations assess, redesign and improve business processes while connecting process improvements to measurable performance outcomes.

What is continuous improvement?

Continuous improvement is a structured approach to identifying, implementing and sustaining incremental improvements in processes, performance and ways of working.
