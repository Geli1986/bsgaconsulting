FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Operational Excellence questions.

What is operational excellence? +

Operational excellence is the structured improvement of the way an organisation works, with the aim of improving efficiency, quality, visibility, productivity and sustainable business performance.

How can BSGA Consulting improve operational performance? +

BSGA Consulting helps organisations identify performance constraints, improve processes, establish meaningful KPIs, strengthen management practices and build continuous improvement capabilities.

Can BSGA Consulting help design operational KPIs? +

Yes. BSGA Consulting can help define meaningful KPIs and reporting structures that give leaders better visibility and support informed operational decisions.

How do you make operational improvements sustainable? +

BSGA focuses on embedding improved processes, behaviours, management practices and continuous improvement methods so operational gains can be maintained over time.
