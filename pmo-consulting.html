PMO Maturity Levels

As organisations grow, their Project Management Office should evolve from basic administrative support to becoming a strategic business capability.

Level 1 – Administrative

The PMO provides document templates and basic reporting but has limited influence on project delivery.

Level 2 – Controlled

Projects follow common methodologies and governance processes, improving consistency across the organisation.

Level 3 – Managed

The PMO provides portfolio reporting, resource coordination and executive oversight that supports better strategic decisions.

Level 4 – Strategic

The PMO actively supports business strategy by prioritising investments, improving governance and maximising portfolio performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PMO?

A Project Management Office (PMO) is a central function that provides governance, standards, reporting and support to improve the successful delivery of projects across an organisation.

Does every organisation need a PMO?

Not every organisation requires a large PMO. The appropriate structure depends on the number, complexity and strategic importance of projects being delivered.

Can you improve an existing PMO?

Yes. We regularly assess existing PMOs, identify capability gaps and recommend practical improvements that strengthen governance, reporting and portfolio management.

Can a PMO support AI and Digital Transformation projects?

Absolutely. Modern PMOs play an essential role in coordinating Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Operational Excellence initiatives while ensuring strategic alignment and executive visibility.

Do you provide interim PMO leadership?

Yes. BSGA Consulting can provide temporary PMO leadership, governance support and executive advisory services during periods of organisational change or major transformation.
