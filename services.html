Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI consulting?

AI consulting helps organisations identify where Artificial Intelligence can create measurable business value and provides the strategy, governance and implementation support needed for successful adoption.

How do I know if my organisation is ready for AI?

Our AI Readiness Assessment evaluates your business processes, technology, data, governance and organisational capabilities before recommending any investment.

Do you implement AI software?

No. BSGA Consulting is an independent consultancy. We help organisations define strategy, manage implementation projects, improve governance and maximise business outcomes while remaining technology neutral.

Which industries do you support?

We support organisations across manufacturing, construction, engineering, energy, oil & gas, chemicals, automotive, retail, consumer goods, telecommunications and professional services.

Can you help with Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT Enterprise?

Yes. We advise organisations on adoption strategy, governance, implementation planning, security considerations, change management and employee enablement for Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT Enterprise.

How long does an AI implementation take?

The duration depends on the complexity of the organisation and the scope of the programme. Most successful initiatives are delivered in phases, allowing value to be realised progressively while reducing implementation risk.
